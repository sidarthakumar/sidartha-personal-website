FROM THE NOTEBOOK / Personal notes

Beginning with curiosity

Oct 10, 20262 min readSidartha Kumar

Updated Oct 18, 2026

This website is a place to bring my work and learning together. My earlier work began in 2009; October 2026 marks this portfolio’s current chapter, rather than the beginning of all my experience in technology.

My background includes approximately five to six years in development, at least four years in SEO, and around six months in testing. These are separate areas of experience, not consecutive periods to add together. Development has included WordPress sites, business websites, online stores, frontend pages, backend systems, and scripts.

A clearer direction

Today I want to deepen my work in full-stack applications and APIs, alongside Python, AI tools, and practical automation. I’m learning across JavaScript, React, Node.js, MySQL, MongoDB, Excel, and Cypress. The skills page describes that current learning alongside my existing background.

The connection between these areas interests me. A website needs to be understandable to its visitors. The application behind it needs to handle information sensibly. Testing helps identify where the experience breaks down. SEO adds another reason to think carefully about structure and useful content.

What I want to share

I intend to publish projects with their context, technical decisions, and current status. A repository link is useful, but an explanation of the problem and the choices behind the work makes it easier for another person to understand.

The learning journey will capture milestones as they happen. The notebook will hold specific observations and practical lessons. Some entries will be small; what matters is that they say something useful about actual work.

This portfolio is the first project documented here. Other work will be added when I have material I can share publicly.
